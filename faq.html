Our certified team handles national portal registration, load feasibility inspection, DISCOM vendor coordination, and document filing to ensure direct subsidy disbursement into your bank account.
How does solar net metering work in Rajasthan?
With an on-grid system, your panels produce electricity during sunny daylight hours. Any surplus electricity your building does not consume is exported to the local electricity board (Jaipur Vidyut Vitran Nigam Limited - JVVNL) grid. A bi-directional "net meter" registers both exported and imported power, directly offsetting your power consumption. MGSS handles the DISCOM liaison, fee submission, and net-meter testing end-to-end.

What tax benefits are available for commercial and industrial businesses?
Commercial entities, factories, and institutions can leverage Accelerated Depreciation (AD) under Section 32 of the Income Tax Act. This enables businesses to write off a substantial portion of the solar system capital cost in the very first year, leading to major corporate tax reductions and a typical payback period of just 3 to 4 years.
